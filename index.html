Warranty terms

Residential products only. Effective June 2025, pump motor cover updated 2026. Full conditions and exclusions apply — see the Waterco Warranty Booklet for complete terms.

12months

In-field labour is covered for 12 months from the date of purchase or installation, within 25 km of an authorised Waterco Service Agent. Outside that radius, or after 12 months, freight and on-site labour are the purchaser's responsibility — the part periods below still apply.

Warranty periods shown are for parts only and apply from the original purchase date by the end user. Spare parts fitted during a repair carry their own fresh 12-month warranty. Some items carry a pro-rata cost-share period after their flat warranty expires — see the "Notes" column. This warranty is not transferable from the original purchaser and is in addition to your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.
